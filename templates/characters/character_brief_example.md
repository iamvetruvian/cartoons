# Sergeant Cole — the discipline that is about to lose (EXAMPLE)

shape_language: Angular and top-heavy; everything points down at the privates.
silhouette: Tall shako + sash diagonal + sword triangle at the hip. Widest at the shoulders.
signature_prop: Sword (never drawn, always tapping).
distinguishing_features:
  1. Tall shako with gold band (only tall hat in the film).
  2. Red sash diagonal across a green coat.
  3. Heavy black moustache, downturned.
posture_or_gait: Stands 4° back, chin down, arms crossed; walks with zero bob — glides like a threat.
face_baseline: 80% stern + 20% neutral, lids at 0.5 (permanent suspicion).
voice_in_face: The slow lid-drop of contempt; the one-sided sneer (cornerL -0.6, cornerR +0.1); shock with jaw intact (open 0.5, corners locked).
crowd_contrast: Privates are round shakos, no sash, bounce 5%; Cole is the only diagonal silhouette on the bridge.
